Features

Everything a desktop downloader does — driven from your browser.

Bulk queue

Paste up to 100 URLs, one per line. The engine downloads up to 8 at a time and lines up the rest automatically.

Live dashboard

Real-time progress bars, download speed, ETA, file sizes and per-video status — no page reloads.

Quality control

Best quality, 4K, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p — or audio-only MP3 extraction with FFmpeg.

Smart retries

Temporary failures and rate limits are retried automatically with exponential backoff. You just watch.

Private by design

Downloads, cookies and files never leave your computer. The website collects nothing.

History & files

Every completed, failed or cancelled job is logged with one-click file access and folder opening.

How it works

Four steps. The website never touches your videos.

1

Install the local engine

FAQ

Is the website itself downloading my videos?

No. The site is static — it can't download anything. It sends your URLs to the small bridge app running on your computer, which does the real work with yt-dlp and FFmpeg. Your videos never pass through any server of ours, because there isn't one.

Do I need to keep the bridge running?

Yes — keep its window (or the EXE) open while you use the Downloader page. The site shows ● Connected when it can reach the bridge at http://127.0.0.1:8765.

Where do my files go?

By default Downloads / Asad Downloader on your own PC. You can change the folder and organize by uploader in Settings.

Does it work on my phone?

The website looks great on mobile, but the actual downloading needs the desktop engine — phones and tablets can't run the yt-dlp + FFmpeg workflow. Use the site on the same computer that runs the bridge.

Is my login / are my cookies sent to you?

Never. If you enable "Use browser cookies" for login-only videos, your browser hands them directly to yt-dlp on your own PC. The website never sees them, and they are redacted from logs.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The site is free static hosting, the engine is free software, and your PC does the work. There is no paid tier because there is no server to pay for.
